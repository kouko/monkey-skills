# Business complexity lens

Use this local lens inside the existing worth-it checkpoint. It does not add a
score, a verdict, or a downstream dependency.

## When it applies

Apply it when a proposal creates a continuing commitment, coordination work,
policy, maintenance obligation, or a material opportunity cost. Prior
project-owned discovery artifacts may inform the assessment when present, but
they are optional evidence: their absence requires an independent local
assessment, not a blocked or weaker verdict.

## Questions

Record the continuing burden for users or operators, who must coordinate, and
which alternative the commitment displaces. State why that burden is worth the
value now. Name work, policy, or coordination that can be removed or avoided,
then identify the downstream product, design, or operational risk that remains.

## Reasoned N/A

Use `Reasoned N/A` only when the already-approved work adds no continuing
business or operational commitment. Say why; a bare `N/A` is not an assessment.
