# Purpose

**Why:** To build a mechanically self-correcting development process — planning, review, evidence, and memory — that any Claude-Code-driven repository can adopt, so that a lesson learned once cannot be silently re-lost and a claim in a plan, spec, or contract cannot ship unverified.

**Done when:** A repository that is not `monkey-skills` installs `loom-code` and `loom-design` cold, runs a real arc through them end to end, and every gate — citation, purpose-link, review-pass, memory-store integrity, open-questions — fires correctly with no repo-specific patching, and no contract text cites a document that repo cannot open.

---

## What this commits to, and what it rules out

Chosen 2026-08-22 from three candidates, on the evidence that 48 of the last 60 commits touch loom directly, that loom is now 24 skills, 5 agents, 163 scripts and 126 released versions of `loom-code` alone, and that `loom-code/scripts/templates/PURPOSE.md` already exports this repo's self-governance mechanism to other repos rather than keeping it private.

**Licensed as first-class bets:** hardening loom's gates, closing the portability gaps that stop a foreign repo adopting it, and the work of separating loom from `monkey-skills` into independently managed repositories.

**Ruled out as first-class bets:** treating feature requests for `investing-toolkit`, `obsidian`, `think-orbit` or the other domain plugins as ends in themselves. They remain valuable, and they remain this repo's proving ground — but under this purpose they earn bet capacity as *evidence about loom*, not as products of their own.

**The honest cost of this choice.** The rejected candidate B ("a personal skill marketplace") described roughly a fifth of recent work — real domain features evaluated on their own merits. Choosing A does not delete that work; it demotes it. If a season passes where the domain plugins are what actually matter, this file is wrong and should be rewritten rather than worked around.

**The standing risk this purpose creates.** Loom's strongest evidence has always come from being tripped by real work — the defects that mattered surfaced while building something else. A purpose that makes loom the product invites loom to be developed against itself, where the only friction is its own. Every bet under this purpose should be able to name the real arc, foreign repo, or measurement that produced its evidence. A bet whose only justification is loom's internal coherence is the failure mode this line exists to catch.
