```markdown
---

## Problem
Project members keep getting interrupted by activity emails from a project
they belong to. The volume of these emails disrupts their work, and they
currently have no way to stop just that project's emails without touching
anything else.

## Proposed outcome
A project member can mute one project so its activity emails stop, while
everything else about how they're notified — in-app notifications for that
project, and all global notification settings — stays exactly as it is
today.

## Acceptance
1. After muting a project, I no longer receive activity emails for that
   project.
2. I still see in-app notifications for that project exactly as before.
3. My global notification settings, and email/in-app notifications for my
   other projects, are unaffected.

## Constraints
- Only the email channel is affected by muting; no other notification
  channel changes.

## Value case
Beneficiary: project members currently interrupted by activity email from
a project they're part of. Urgency: the interruption is ongoing and named
as the specific pain (email volume), not a hypothetical. GO — clear,
recurring pain with a narrow fix.

## Out of scope
- Unmuting a previously muted project.
- Any change to in-app notification behavior.
- Any change to global or account-level notification settings.
- Muting notification channels other than email.

## Open questions
- none
```
