## Problem
Members of a project keep getting interrupted by activity email from that
project. There is currently no way to reduce just those emails without
turning off notifications more broadly.

## Proposed outcome
A project member can mute one project so its activity no longer generates
email for them, while everything else about how they're notified — in-app
notifications for that project, and their global notification settings —
stays exactly as it is today. Email for their other, non-muted projects is
unaffected.

## Acceptance
1. After a member mutes a project, no further activity email arrives for
   that project.
2. In-app notifications for that project keep arriving unchanged.
3. The member's global notification settings remain unchanged.
4. Activity email for the member's other (non-muted) projects keeps
   arriving unchanged.

## Constraints
- Only the email channel is affected by muting a project; nothing else
  about notification delivery changes.

## Out of scope
- Un-muting a project (reverting it back to sending email) is not part of
  this change.

## Value case
Beneficiary: project members currently interrupted by repeated activity
email from one project. Urgency: the interruptions are ongoing today.
GO — confirmed by the user.

## Open questions
- none
